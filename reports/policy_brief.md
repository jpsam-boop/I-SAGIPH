# I-SAGIPH policy brief: where to act first

**187 cities and municipalities** are priority areas (high health need, low hospital capacity) in at least 80% of 1,000 model variations, home to **7.2 million people**. Nationally, 38% of residents are modelled as more than 30 minutes from a hospital, and beds within reach average 1.12 per 1,000 against a DOH minimum of 1.25.

## What holds each priority area back

| bottleneck | areas | people | beds_short | action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Distance | 184 | 6,981,523 | 7,778 | Bring care closer: a new Level 1 hospital or infirmary, referral transport, or telemedicine |
| Bed supply | 3 | 186,045 | 191 | Add beds where people already travel: expand or upgrade an existing hospital (Level 1 to Level 2) |

Also: 187 of these areas have most residents more than 60 minutes from a Malasakit Center (candidates under R.A. 11463), and 184 have weak licensed primary care.
